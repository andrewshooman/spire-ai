# Slay the Spire playbook

## Core principles
- HP is the run's main resource. Spend it for power (elites, risky events) only when the deck can take the fight.
- Do not force an archetype. Early picks build a foundation that survives Acts 1-2: front-loaded damage, area damage, reliable block, draw. Steer toward synergies only once the right pieces show up, and value cards that combo with what the deck already has. Play the deck the Spire gives you.
- Quality beats size. A card must beat the average card already in the deck. Skipping a card reward is often right once the core pieces are in.
- Card roles to cover: Strike-plus (a Strike with a bonus: Pommel Strike, Twin Strike), Block-plus (Shrug It Off), front-loaded damage (Carnage, Bludgeon, Hemokinesis), area damage (Cleave, Thunderclap, Whirlwind, Immolate), draw/cycle, combo pieces, and engines (Demon Form, Corruption, Catalyst, Electrodynamics).
- Act 1: damage first (one Strike-plus, one area card, one big hitter), then defense late in the act. Act 2: find scaling (Strength, poison, X-cost, relic synergies). Act 3: patch weaknesses and prepare for the known boss.

## Neow
- If the last run did not reach the Act 1 boss, only Max HP or "first three enemies have 1 HP" are offered.
- "Enemies in your next three combats have 1 HP" is very strong: route into an early elite for a free relic.
- Random common relic: safe and consistent. Random rare relic with a downside: usually worth it (Dead Branch-level relics win runs).
- Random rare card: high variance; good when it is immediately playable (Wraith Form, Electrodynamics), bad when it is a slow combo piece.
- Swap starter relic for a random boss relic: volatile. Ironclad loses Burning Blood healing; Silent loses Ring of the Snake (worse); Defect cares least.
- Remove/transform a Strike or Defend, or 250 gold for max HP, are solid. Upgrading one card is the weakest.

## Combat
- Plan the whole turn: sum the damage of the cards you can afford against the enemy's block, block only what the intents demand, spend all energy.
- Kill order: enemies that buff others or scale first, then the lowest-HP enemy to cut incoming damage.
- Apply Vulnerable before big attacks and Weak to the biggest attacker. Strength reduction (Disarm, Piercing Wail, Dark Shackles) blocks a lot against multi-hits.
- Play powers early in long fights (elites, bosses), not when you are about to die.
- Potions: never die holding one; in boss fights use them early; in elites one opener; Fruit Juice right away.

## Map, rest sites and shops
- Act 1: take 1-2 elites when HP is above about 60% and the deck has front-loaded damage. With the 1-HP Neow blessing, route into an elite immediately.
- Act 3: if the deck is shaky avoid elites, take fights and rest sites, use shops to patch holes; a strong deck can take the rich Act 3 events.
- Before a boss, rest unless HP is high; otherwise upgrade the card that matters most. Ironclad: upgrade Bash early (more Vulnerable).
- Shops: removing a Strike or curse is almost always good; buy key cards and strong relics; before the Act 3 boss do not leave without potions (Fairy in a Bottle, Entropic Brew, Strength/Dexterity).

## Events
- Prefer relics, card removal and upgrades; avoid curses unless the reward is big and the deck can absorb it.
- Pay HP only with HP to spare (above about 60%) and when the prize helps the plan.
- Act 3 events reward strong decks; the lows can be terrible, so play them safer with a shaky deck.

## Boss relics
- Energy relics are strong; check each downside against the deck.
- Snecko Eye: great with many 2+ cost cards, then build around it. Velvet Choker: fine for expensive decks that play few cards a turn, otherwise avoid.
- Calling Bell: three relics and three curses; a jackpot with Omamori. Astrolabe: transform and upgrade three cards; strong with many Strikes/Defends left.
- Philosopher's Stone: +1 Strength to enemies is manageable with Weak, Disarm or Piercing Wail; the energy is huge (best for Silent).
- Coffee Dripper: no resting; good with Burning Blood/Black Blood or other healing (Reaper, Feed, Self Repair).
- Fusion Hammer: no smithing; good when the deck is mostly upgraded or has other upgrade sources (Armaments, Anvil).
- Tiny House is the weakest; take it only when the others hurt the deck. Runic Dome hides intents.

## Act 1
- Elites are damage races. Gremlin Nob: every Skill you play gives it Strength, so attack hard and fast; Weak blunts its hits. Lagavulin: sleeps for two turns, so set up powers and cycle, then burst; the fight becomes a race before its Strength/Dexterity drain cripples you. Sentries: kill one quickly (area damage is ideal) before Dazed cards clog the deck; the middle one attacks turn 1, the outer two turn 2.
- Slime Boss: damage race; get it below half fast and as low as possible before the split so the slimes are small; area damage for the second phase.
- Hexaghost: needs damage growth (Inflame, Spot Weakness, Rupture with its burns, Paper Phrog + Vulnerable, Anger + draw); potions can stand in (Cultist, Strength, Thorns). Its turn-2 attack hits 6 times for (current HP / 12) each, so high HP is less valuable here: 35 HP takes 18, 36 HP takes 24.
- The Guardian: attrition. It alternates offense and a Defensive Mode with Sharp Hide (attacking hurts you); block and set up in that mode, burst in the openings. Flame Barrier, Caltrops and big block cards shine.

## Act 2
- Needs scaling by now. Byrds want multi-hits; Man-Eating Plant and Snake Plant punish multi-hit chip; Shelled Parasite punishes stalling; Triple Cultists need a fast kill or area damage; Spheric Guardian keeps its block (Barricade), so keep attacking through it.
- Book of Stabbing: damage check whose multi-hit grows; beat it fast, Weak and Strength reduction help. Gremlin Leader: kill the leader or control the minions with area damage. Slavers: kill the Red Slaver (the net) first, then the rest.
- The Champ: chip toward half HP but do not cross it until ready; after the transform he is hyper-aggressive, so unload. The Collector: area damage; do not bother killing both Torch Heads; end it before the debuffs make her hits 30-40. Bronze Automaton: kill an Orb to get a stolen card back; every seventh turn Hyper Beam hits for about 60, so either burst it first or prepare to survive that turn; it has 3 Artifact.

## Act 3
- Hallway fights rival elites: Exploders blow up after three turns, Spikers reflect damage (avoid multi-hit chip into them), Darklings revive unless killed together, Transient fades after five turns (just survive), Spire Growth constricts.
- Elites: Giant Head counts up damage with each card played, so play setup first and big hits last and kill it before its countdown ends. Nemesis is Intangible every other turn: set up then, unload on the vulnerable turns. Reptomancer: kill the daggers at once, area damage is king.
- Awakened One: kill the Cultists first; every Power played in phase 1 gives it Strength, so hold Powers until phase 2, then play them all. Donu and Deca: kill Donu first; Malaise or Weak shuts down the survivor. Time Eater: 12 cards ends your turn and gives it Strength; aim for 5-6 cards a turn, never end a turn on 10-11; it heals to half once, so stop just above half unless you can burst through; Flame Barrier punishes its multi-hits.

## Act 4
- Needs all three keys: Red (beat a burning elite), Blue (rest-site key instead of resting), Green (take it instead of a chest relic).
- Spear and Shield: kill the Shield first; the one you do not face hits harder.
- The Heart: every card you play costs HP (Beat of Death); damage is capped at 300 a turn; it ramps forever, so build a win condition and defensive scaling, reliable draw, Weak and Strength reduction for its multi-hits, and bring the best potions.

## Ironclad
- Burning Blood heals 6 after every combat, so Ironclad can afford more fights early.
- Strong early damage: Carnage, Hemokinesis, Pommel Strike, Twin Strike, Uppercut, Headbutt, Bludgeon. Area: Cleave, Thunderclap, Immolate, Whirlwind.
- Strength: Inflame, Spot Weakness, Demon Form, Limit Break (only with Strength) with multi-hit payoffs Heavy Blade, Sword Boomerang, Whirlwind, Twin Strike, Pummel.
- Exhaust: Corruption + Feel No Pain + Dark Embrace; enablers Burning Pact, Second Wind, True Grit, Fiend Fire, Sever Soul.
- Block: Barricade + Entrench + Body Slam; support Impervious, Flame Barrier, Metallicize, Shrug It Off, Juggernaut.
- Self-damage: Rupture with Hemokinesis, Offering, Combust, Bloodletting, Brutality; Reaper for sustain.

## Silent
- Poison: Noxious Fumes, Deadly Poison, Bouncing Flask, Catalyst, Corpse Explosion; needs block while it ticks.
- Shivs: Blade Dance, Cloak and Dagger, Accuracy, After Image, Infinite Blades. Discard: Tactician, Reflex, Acrobatics, Calculated Gamble, Eviscerate.
- Defense: Footwork, Blur, Backflip, Leg Sweep, Piercing Wail, Malaise. Wraith Form and Well-Laid Plans are strong.

## Defect
- Focus is the key scaling stat: Defragment, Biased Cognition, Consume. Frost defense: Glacier, Coolheaded, Loop, Capacitor.
- Lightning: Ball Lightning, Electrodynamics, Thunder Strike, Static Discharge. Zero-cost: Claw, All for One, Scrape, FTL. Echo Form, Creative AI for long fights.

## Watcher
- Stance dance: Eruption into Wrath for damage, Vigilance or Calm to exit; leave Wrath before enemies attack unless the hit is lethal. Tantrum, Flurry of Blows, Mental Fortress, Rushdown.
- Scry and retain: Third Eye, Cut Through Fate, Sanctity, Wallop, Ragnarok. Divinity: Prostrate, Worship, Devotion, Blasphemy.
