# Slay the Spire playbook

## Core principles
- HP is the run's main resource. Spend it for power (elites, risky events) only when the deck can take the fight.
- Build the deck as one machine: an archetype engine, enough front-loaded damage, enough block, and draw or energy to run it. Judge every card offer, shop purchase, removal, upgrade and boss relic by how it serves the RUN PLAN and the whole deck, never by the card alone.
- Quality beats size. A card must beat the average card already in the deck. Skipping a card reward is often right, especially once the core pieces are in.
- Act 1: pick up 2-3 strong damage cards early so elites and the boss die fast, then start adding scaling. Remove Strikes when you can.
- Act 2: needs area damage (Gremlin Leader, Slavers, Byrds, Chosen) and enough block or scaling for long fights.
- Act 3: needs real scaling and a boss plan.

## Combat
- Read every intent. When attacked, block what you need, not more. When enemies are not attacking, go all-in on damage or setup.
- Kill order: enemies that buff others or scale first, then the lowest HP enemy to cut incoming damage.
- Apply Vulnerable before big attacks and Weak to the biggest attacker.
- Play powers early in long fights (elites, bosses), not when you are about to die.
- Spend all your energy. Play 0-cost and draw cards first so you see more options.
- Potions are free power: use them in elite and boss fights or to avoid big HP loss. Never die holding potions.

## Map, rest sites and shops
- Act 1: take 1-2 elites if HP is above about 60% and the deck has front-loaded damage; prefer paths with rest sites before elites and the boss.
- Rest when HP is under about 50% before a dangerous fight; otherwise upgrade (Smith) the card that matters most to the plan.
- Shops: removing a Strike or curse is almost always good; buy key archetype cards and strong relics. Arrive with 150+ gold when possible.
- Boss relics: energy relics are strong; check each downside against the plan.

## Ironclad
- Burning Blood heals 6 after every combat, so Ironclad can afford more fights early.
- Strong early damage: Carnage, Hemokinesis, Pommel Strike, Twin Strike, Uppercut, Headbutt, Bludgeon. Area damage: Cleave, Thunderclap, Immolate, Whirlwind.
- Strength: Inflame, Spot Weakness, Demon Form, Limit Break (only once you have Strength) with multi-hit payoffs Heavy Blade, Sword Boomerang, Whirlwind, Twin Strike, Pummel.
- Exhaust: Corruption + Feel No Pain + Dark Embrace engine; enablers Burning Pact, Second Wind, True Grit, Fiend Fire, Sever Soul.
- Block: Barricade + Entrench + Body Slam; support Impervious, Flame Barrier, Metallicize, Shrug It Off, Juggernaut.
- Self-damage: Rupture with Hemokinesis, Offering, Combust, Bloodletting, Brutality; Reaper for sustain.
- Good in almost any Ironclad deck: Shrug It Off, Offering, Battle Trance, Pommel Strike, Feed, Disarm.

## Silent
- Poison: Noxious Fumes, Deadly Poison, Bouncing Flask, Catalyst, Corpse Explosion; needs block while it ticks.
- Shivs: Blade Dance, Cloak and Dagger, Accuracy, After Image, Infinite Blades.
- Discard: Tactician, Reflex, Acrobatics, Calculated Gamble, Eviscerate.
- Defense core: Footwork, Blur, Backflip, Leg Sweep, Piercing Wail. Wraith Form and Well-Laid Plans are strong.

## Defect
- Focus is the key scaling stat: Defragment, Biased Cognition, Consume.
- Frost defense: Glacier, Coolheaded, Loop, Capacitor.
- Lightning damage: Ball Lightning, Electrodynamics, Thunder Strike, Static Discharge.
- Zero-cost: Claw, All for One, Scrape, FTL. Echo Form and Creative AI for long fights.

## Watcher
- Stance dance: Eruption into Wrath for damage, Vigilance or Calm to exit; Tantrum, Flurry of Blows, Mental Fortress, Rushdown. Leave Wrath before enemies attack unless the hit is lethal.
- Scry and retain: Third Eye, Cut Through Fate, Sanctity, Wallop, Ragnarok.
- Divinity: Prostrate, Worship, Devotion, Blasphemy.

## Bosses
- The Guardian: after taking enough damage it shifts to Defensive Mode with Sharp Hide (attacking it hurts you); block its heavy attacks and use skills then.
- Hexaghost: turn 2 Divider scales with your current HP, then Inferno and burns; bring front-loaded damage and block on big turns.
- Slime Boss: splits at half HP into two slimes with its current HP; a big hit just before the split makes the halves small. Area damage helps.
- Act 2: Bronze Automaton (kill or block through Hyper Beam), The Champ (gains Strength at half HP), The Collector (area damage for the minions).
- Act 3: Awakened One punishes playing powers in phase 1; Time Eater ends your turn after 12 cards; Donu and Deca need scaling and block.
